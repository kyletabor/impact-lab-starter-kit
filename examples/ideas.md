# Starter ideas

Stuck? Steal one of these, or better, ask your small business owner: **"What did you do last week that you hated doing?"** That answer beats every idea on this page.

These are made-up examples. Each one names a real kind of business, a chore owners complain about, and a demo you could finish in a day.

| Business | The chore | One-day demo |
|---|---|---|
| Bakery or cafe | Turning a week of orders into a prep list | Upload an orders spreadsheet, get a printable prep sheet by day |
| Hair or nail salon | Last-minute no-shows | Paste tomorrow's bookings, get friendly reminder texts to copy and send |
| Plumber or handyman | Writing quotes at night after the job | Type job notes, get a clean quote with line items and a total |
| Restaurant | Replying to online reviews | Paste a review, get a reply in the owner's voice, warm for good ones and calm for bad ones |
| Gift or retail shop | Running out of best sellers | Upload a sales spreadsheet, see what to reorder this week and why |
| Bookkeeping or tax prep | Clients sending messy receipts | Drop in receipt photos, get rows for a spreadsheet with date, vendor and amount |
| Dog walker or cleaner | Planning tomorrow's route | Paste the client list, get the visits in a sensible order with times |
| Any small shop | Writing the weekly social post | Answer three questions, get a week of posts with captions |

## What makes a strong hackathon idea

- **One person, one chore.** "Helps the salon front desk confirm tomorrow's bookings" beats "AI for salons."
- **Any shop can use it.** Another bakery should be able to clone your repo and run it with their own spreadsheet.
- **Works with what they already have.** A spreadsheet, a photo, copy and paste. No new system to learn.
- **The owner checks the work.** Claude drafts; the owner approves before anything goes to a customer.
- **You can show it in 2 minutes.** Before, a messy chore. After, done in seconds.
